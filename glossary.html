Glossary

The terms Situ uses, each in a sentence, with the page that explains it

Each definition repeats what a page already says. Follow the link for the full account.

Space syntax

Term What it means here More
space syntax A way of measuring where each street sits in the whole street network, begun by Bill Hillier and Julienne Hanson. What space syntax is
segment A piece of street between two junctions. S1
depth The distance between two segments, counted by how much you turn: a right angle costs 1, a 45° turn 0.5. Segments and depth
angular segment analysis The kind of space syntax analysis Situ runs, with depth counted in turns. The walking network
integration How close a segment is, on average, to every other segment: how easy a street is to reach. Integration and choice
choice How often a segment lies on the shortest routes between every pair of segments: how often a street is passed through. Integration and choice
NAIN, NACH Normalised integration and normalised choice, rescaled so that networks of different sizes can be compared. Integration and choice
radius How far the analysis looks around each segment, measured along the streets. Radius n has no limit, which here means the whole island. Radius
natural movement The share of walking the layout alone produces, as Hillier and colleagues argued in 1993. Natural movement
PlanX The QGIS tool that computes the values; Situ used version 4.19.0. The walking network

The network

Term What it means here More
junction Where segment ends meet. S1
edge The streets just outside the study area’s boundary that touch it. S1
gate Where the study area meets the edge. S1
exit The far end of an edge street, where the city carries on. S1
dead end A junction with only one segment that is neither a gate nor an exit. S1
connector A short straight line I added where a path end and a street do not quite touch in the data. Every one is listed. The walking network
checkpoint A place where I doubted the data. Each one has an id and is listed in the Lab, settled or still open. Checks and decisions

What S8 and S9 measure

Term What it means here More
isovist The part of the city visible from one spot (Benedikt 1979). S8
compactness 1 for a round view, smaller as the view stretches into a corridor. S8
reading One version of S9’s imagined morning, changing a single rule (the route), so a difference between readings points at that rule. S9
ledger The list of every rule S9 adds, each with its source or marked as an assumption. S9 build
rank correlation How alike two orders are: 1 means the same order. S8
census tract One of the small areas the census reports on. S7
lidar A survey by laser from an aircraft: each return is a point with its height. Québec’s survey of 2023-11-21 gives most of Situ’s building heights. S4: heights, by source
surface model, terrain model A grid of heights: the surface model includes buildings, the terrain model is the bare ground. A building’s height is the one minus the other inside its outline. S4: heights, by source
one storey The tolerance within which two heights of one building agree: 3.5 m, the median height per storey the 2023 lidar measures. S4: heights, by source

From systems to a game

Term What it means here More
NPC A non-player character: a person or a vehicle the game moves by its own rules. From systems to a game
translation ledger The list of decisions that turn a system into game elements, each with its kind and an alternative. G1 World
local frame The coordinates G1 hands to a game: metres from one origin at the study area’s south-west corner. G1 World
base and parts How G1 draws an outline that holds a podium and a tower: the outline at a base height, and each measured part standing on it at its own height. The parts are mapped in OpenStreetMap, modelled in 2016, or, where neither exists, height zones cut from the 2023 lidar. G1 World
discarded height A height another source gives a building that lies more than one storey from the newest measurement. G1 keeps the newest and says why it set the other aside; S4 keeps every value. G1 World
feature record For each thing built in the game: its source, input, transformation, output, how it was verified, and its limits. The game, stage by stage
candidate point A place where people could stop or gather, marked from the systems. No source records the gathering itself. From systems to a game

How sure, and from where

Term What it means here More
research-creation Research and making that answer each other. In Situ the systems are the research, made to inform a game, which is the making. About
verified The data or a test shows it. A system is verified when it is built and its tests pass. About
partial Some evidence supports it, and a gap remains. About
planned Not built yet. About
assumption A reading of mine, or a rule I chose because no source settles it. About
run record The file each system writes beside its results, with a fingerprint of every input and output. Tracing a result
Mapillary A site where people and the city upload street photos; its software finds objects in them. S6
tax roll The city’s official list of every property and what it is. S4
OpenStreetMap The open map anyone can edit. S4
