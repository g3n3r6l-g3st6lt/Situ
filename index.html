Situ

A prototype for my PhD research-creation project. It asks whether Griffintown's spatial culture and urban rhythm can be mediated and archived through a game-like 3D experience, built one small system at a time from the city's open data and from observation.

Each system has one job. I draw it as a blueprint and test it, then try it in the Lab, an interactive map with every system on it, before the next system may use it.

  • About Situ

Method

Space syntax is one of my measuring sticks, beside the city's own counts. The simulated people never read it. I compare where they went with what it predicts, street by street.

Integration measures how easy a street is to get to. Choice measures how often it lies on the way between other places.

Data

One walking network for the whole island: the city's streets joined with the off-street paths people use.

47,197 lines

I removed highways and unbuilt streets and added 780 of the city's own paths.

Lab

Every system on one map, each with sliders to try. Colour the streets by one system and lay the others on top.

The Lab replays the results of each system, and its own tests check that it shows them exactly.
