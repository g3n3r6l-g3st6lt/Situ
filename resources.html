A list of the files I downloaded, and of the layers I made from them in QGIS, gives each one’s date and licence. For the files I saved for Sample 4, the date is the date of the file on my computer, since I did not record the download. The study area outline is one I drew following Google Maps’ outline of Griffintown, and the analysis ran in PlanX 4.19.0. The Concordia NGCI building file is listed there too. It has no licence, so S4 does not use it.

Readings

Space syntax

  • Hillier, B., & Hanson, J. (1984). The Social Logic of Space. Cambridge University Press.
  • Hillier, B., Penn, A., Hanson, J., Grajewski, T., & Xu, J. (1993). Natural movement: or, configuration and attraction in urban pedestrian movement. Environment and Planning B, 20(1), 29–66.
  • Hillier, B. (1996). Space is the Machine. Cambridge University Press.
  • Ratti, C. (2004). Space syntax: some inconsistencies. Environment and Planning B, 31(4), 487–499.
  • Hillier, B., & Iida, S. (2005). Network and psychological effects in urban movement. In Spatial Information Theory (COSIT 2005), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 3693, 475–490. Springer.
  • Turner, A. (2007). From axial to road-centre lines: a new representation for space syntax and a new model of route choice for transport network analysis. Environment and Planning B, 34(3), 539–555.
  • Hillier, B., Yang, T., & Turner, A. (2012). Normalising least angle choice in Depthmap and how it opens up new perspectives on the global and local analysis of city space. Journal of Space Syntax, 3(2), 155–193.
  • van Nes, A., & Yamu, C. (2021). Introduction to Space Syntax in Urban Studies. Springer. Open access.

Spatial culture and rhythm

  • Griffiths, S., & von Lünen, A. (Eds.) (2016). Spatial Cultures: Towards a New Social Morphology of Cities Past and Present. Routledge.
  • Adams, R. (2024). Designing Spatial Culture. Routledge.
  • Lefebvre, H. (1991). The Production of Space (D. Nicholson-Smith, Trans.). Blackwell. (Original work published 1974)
  • Lefebvre, H. (2004). Rhythmanalysis: Space, Time and Everyday Life (S. Elden & G. Moore, Trans.). Continuum. (Original work published 1992)

Archives and heritage visualisation

  • Schellenberg, T. R. (2003). Modern Archives: Principles and Techniques. Society of American Archivists. (Original work published 1956)
  • Taylor, D. (2003). The Archive and the Repertoire: Performing Cultural Memory in the Americas. Duke University Press.
  • Denard, H. (Ed.) (2009). The London Charter for the Computer-Based Visualisation of Cultural Heritage, version 2.1. london-charter.org
  • López-Menchero, V. M., & Grande, A. (2011). The principles of the Seville Charter. Proceedings of the XXIII CIPA Symposium, Prague.

Data and the city

  • Gitelman, L. (Ed.) (2013). “Raw Data” Is an Oxymoron. MIT Press.
  • Kitchin, R. (2014). The Data Revolution: Big Data, Open Data, Data Infrastructures and Their Consequences. SAGE.
  • Offenhuber, D., & Ratti, C. (Eds.) (2014). Decoding the City: Urbanism in the Age of Big Data. Birkhäuser.

Traffic

  • Webster, F. V. (1958). Traffic Signal Settings. Road Research Technical Paper 39. Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
  • Ville de Montréal (2007). Assemblée publique sur la révision du réseau artériel de Montréal. Commission permanente sur le transport, 12 April 2007. PDF
  • Federal Highway Administration (2023). Highway Functional Classification: Concepts, Criteria and Procedures. U.S. Department of Transportation. PDF

Isovists

  • Benedikt, M. L. (1979). To take hold of space: isovists and isovist fields. Environment and Planning B, 6(1), 47–65.
  • Batty, M. (2001). Exploring isovist fields: space and shape in architectural and urban morphology. Environment and Planning B, 28(1), 123–150.
  • Turner, A., Doxa, M., O’Sullivan, D., & Penn, A. (2001). From isovists to visibility graphs: a methodology for the analysis of architectural space. Environment and Planning B, 28(1), 103–121.

People, public space and movement (for the game)

  • Gehl, J. (2011). Life Between Buildings: Using Public Space (J. Koch, Trans.). Island Press. (Original work published 1971)
  • Hägerstrand, T. (1970). What about people in regional science? Papers of the Regional Science Association, 24, 7–21.
  • Seamon, D. (1979). A Geography of the Lifeworld: Movement, Rest and Encounter. Croom Helm.
  • Treiber, M., Hennecke, A., & Helbing, D. (2000). Congested traffic states in empirical observations and microscopic simulations. Physical Review E, 62(2), 1805–1824.
  • Whyte, W. H. (1980). The Social Life of Small Urban Spaces. The Conservation Foundation.

These are cited on the game pages without page numbers, and are not yet checked in Reading closely.

Network measures

  • Sabidussi, G. (1966). The centrality index of a graph. Psychometrika, 31(4), 581–603.
  • Freeman, L. C. (1977). A set of measures of centrality based on betweenness. Sociometry, 40(1), 35–41.
